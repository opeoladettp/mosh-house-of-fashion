"True luxury lies in the provenance of the fiber and the precision of the cut."

Adekunle O. Moshood | Founder

With an academic foundation in Agronomy specializing in natural plant fibers and eco-textiles from Ladoke Akintola University of Technology, Adekunle brings a rare scientific precision to luxury fashion.

MOSH House of Fashion bridges artisanal Nigerian handlooms—such as organic Yoruba Aso-Oke woven with heritage cotton and natural indigo Adire vats—with architectural British tailoring patterns. The result is structured menswear that honors ancestral artistry while commanding global boardrooms and red carpets.

The MOSH Atelier Commission Workflow

Every bespoke garment is an intimate collaboration between the patron and the master artisan, taking over 60 hours of specialized handwork.

✻
01

Textile Curation & Silhouette

Initial consultation to select bespoke fabric weights, handloom weave patterns, organic dye tones, and garment purpose.

02

Anatomical Pattern Drafting

Over 32 precise anatomical measurements taken to draft a unique individual 2D blueprint tailored to your posture.

03

Hand-Canvassing & Baste Fitting

Hand-stitching horsehair canvas, floating chest pieces, and intermediate baste fitting for micro-adjustments.

04

Final Delivery & Archiving

Hand-finished buttonholes, horn buttons, custom interior monogramming, and bespoke garment suit-bag delivery.
